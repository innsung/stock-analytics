# V3.2.1 Phase 5.57 — Subsidiary Action Applicability

## Outcome

Acquired OpenDART documents were parsed for an explicit statement that the
action issuer is a separate subsidiary or child company. Such actions do not
mechanically issue, reduce, split, or distribute the listed parent's security
and are emitted as NOT_APPLICABLE evidence for the parent-level total-return
queue.

Related-party participation notices are kept for direct issuer review because
the listed company itself may be conducting the offering. Ambiguous disclosure
matches remain unresolved.
