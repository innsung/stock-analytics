# V3.2.1 Phase 5.67 — Recent Dividend Evidence Inventory

## Outcome

The 17 recent annual-report dividend facts were re-audited against previously
collected strict market events. Later 2026 dividend events are not treated as
evidence for March 2025 annual-report rows merely because the listed code matches.

Each row now has a corrected historical search window extending 450 days before
and 30 days after its disclosure date. Evidence outside that window is recorded
as non-linkable and no row is auto-promoted.
