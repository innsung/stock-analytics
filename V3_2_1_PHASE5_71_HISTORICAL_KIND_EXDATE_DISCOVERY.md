# V3.2.1 Phase 5.71 Historical KIND Ex-date Discovery

The 11 PIT-eligible historical dividend candidates are searched against official KIND market-action notices. Company names come from the current OpenDART corporation-code archive, and both individual and aggregate notice bodies are checked for the target company.

Discovery records official KIND receipt/document identifiers and source URLs. It intentionally does not promote an event until the notice body date is parsed and validated in the next phase.
