# V3.2.1 Phase 5.84 Historical Legal Event Chain

Phase 5.84 maps the 92 historical amendments, attachments, and completion reports to unique OpenDART child receipts and candidate primary decisions.

Duplicate disclosure-list rows are collapsed by receipt number. Parent candidates require the same listed code and mechanic family, a preceding date within two years, and preferably an identical normalized legal title. Nearest-date selection is only a candidate link. No row is resolved until the original child document proves its relationship and the primary event mechanics are validated.
