# V3.2.1 Phase 5.86 Historical Chain Consolidation

Phase 5.86 consolidates the 52 document-confirmed amendments, attachments, and completion reports into 30 primary legal-event groups. Confirmed child disclosures are not independent adjustment events and may be marked `NOT_APPLICABLE` only because their original documents and parent receipts remain traceable.

For each group, the latest confirmed amendment or attachment is preserved as the controlling mechanics document. Completion reports never replace the controlling decision. Every primary event remains unresolved until its effective date and adjustment mechanics are validated.
