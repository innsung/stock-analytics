# V3.2.1 Phase 5.88 Primary Adjustment Market Validation

Phase 5.88 audits the 45 extracted official-date candidates against point-in-time ordering, the research cutoff, and the stored trading calendar.

Only legally simple share splits or consolidations may be auto-validated from a unique KRX adjusted/raw price-ratio breakpoint. Rights offerings, mergers, spinoffs, share exchanges, and capital reductions remain unresolved because a price discontinuity alone cannot prove their economic adjustment factor.
