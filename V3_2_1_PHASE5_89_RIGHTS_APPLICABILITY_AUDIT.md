# V3.2.1 Phase 5.89 Rights Applicability Audit

Phase 5.89 checks whether the 17 extracted rights-offering candidates actually create rights for shareholders of the listed security.

Subsidiary issuances do not adjust the listed parent's shares. General public offerings and third-party allotments do not create an existing-shareholder rights ex-date. Only an original document explicitly proving shareholder allotment may proceed to theoretical ex-rights price validation.
