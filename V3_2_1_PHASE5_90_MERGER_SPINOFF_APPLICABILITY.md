# V3.2.1 Phase 5.90 Merger and Spinoff Applicability

Phase 5.90 separates historical restructurings that cannot mechanically adjust the listed security from material events requiring valuation.

Subsidiary restructurings, explicit no-new-share mergers, pre-listing events, and physical spinoffs without shareholder distributions may be excluded. Human spinoffs, mergers issuing consideration shares, and documents with invalid extracted date order remain unresolved.
