# V3.2.1 Phase 6.03 — Samsung Heavy preferred-share delisting warnings

This phase reviews two KRX investor-warning disclosures concerning Samsung Heavy Industries' first preferred share, not its common share (`010140`).

Promotion is fail-closed: each queue row must map to one exact KRX disclosure receipt, and the retrieved document and inventory title must explicitly identify the first preferred share, delisting warning, and the distinct common-share security. Passing warnings are `NOT_APPLICABLE` to the common-share total-return series and do not create a price or unit adjustment.
