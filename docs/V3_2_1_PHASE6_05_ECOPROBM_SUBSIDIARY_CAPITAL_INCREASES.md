# V3.2.1 Phase 6.05 — Ecopro BM subsidiary capital increases

This phase resolves seven queue rows across four disclosure dates for capital increases by Ecopro BM subsidiaries. Dates containing two economically distinct subsidiary filings are validated as complete receipt groups, avoiding arbitrary queue-to-receipt pairing where the queue descriptions are identical.

Every DART document must identify a subsidiary management matter, capital increase, subsidiary issuer, and new-share terms. Any language granting shares directly to Ecopro BM shareholders blocks promotion. Passing rows are `NOT_APPLICABLE` to the listed parent's total-return series.
