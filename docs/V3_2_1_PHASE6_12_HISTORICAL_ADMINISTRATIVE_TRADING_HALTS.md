# V3.2.1 Phase 6.12 — Historical administrative trading halts

Four KRX halt notices are isolated from their underlying disclosures: SK hynix and Celltrion business-transfer announcements, Ecopro BM's supply contract, and Hanmi Semiconductor's treasury-share cancellation announcement.

Each receipt must explicitly state the halt and its expected reason. These notice rows apply no shares or cash and are `NOT_APPLICABLE` as additional total-return events; the underlying legal events remain independently auditable.
