# V3.2.1 Phase 6.13 — Related-party rights participation follow-ups

Five participation notices are tied to two underlying rights offerings: three Samsung Heavy Industries affiliates participated in its 250,000,000-share offering, and two Samsung affiliates participated in Samsung Biologics' 5,009,000-share offering.

These notices disclose who subscribed; they do not create another distribution or adjustment. They are `NOT_APPLICABLE` only as duplicate follow-up rows. The underlying rights offerings remain independently auditable and are explicitly not resolved by this phase.
