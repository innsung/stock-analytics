# V3.2.1 Phase 6.16 — Physical-split and business-transfer completions

Kakao, LG Chem, and HD Hyundai completion reports describe physical splits whose new-company shares were allocated 100% to the parent company, not listed shareholders. Celltrion's business-transfer report states no new shares or cash consideration.

Five rows are therefore `NOT_APPLICABLE` to listed-holder total return. The audit explicitly distinguishes these physical splits from shareholder-distributing spin-offs.
