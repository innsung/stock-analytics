# V3.2.1 Phase 6.18 — Rights-offering follow-up disclosures

Phase 6.18 removes eight exact, official rights-offering follow-up disclosures that do not create an additional shareholder return event.

- Ecopro BM: final price, subscription result, and issuance result (3)
- Samsung Biologics: corrected decisions, price notice, subscription result, and issuance result (5)
- Exact queue identity and OpenDART receipt/title/date are required; mismatches fail closed.
- Market adjustment notices such as an ex-rights event are deliberately excluded and remain unresolved until strict effective-date and factor evidence is available.
