# V3.2.1 Phase 6.24 — HD KSOE third-party capital raise

The 2021-06-30 correction describes a third-party convertible/redeemable preferred capital raise, not an existing-shareholder rights distribution. OpenDART structured fields lock the common/preferred issuance counts, preferred issue price, named third-party participant, and empty existing-holder allotment. Therefore it creates no ex-rights or holder-unit event. All identity and term checks fail closed.
